about

I build first.
Theorize later.

Working code teaches me things no paper ever could. I hack fast, ship fast, then obsess over every layer of why it actually worked.

That's how CodeSensei was born: five agents that had to agree on one coherent answer. The hard part wasn't building them — it was making them not contradict each other.

current obsession

Building an LLM from scratch. Not fine-tuning. Not APIs. Every transformer block, every attention head, every gradient update — every single line of code, mine. Every weight, trained by me. Small model. Total ownership.

belief

AI is the biggest wave in human history and it's already moving. The only way to not get swept away is to understand it from within. No shortcuts. No wrappers. No skipping the hard parts.

what I don't build
1. ChatGPT wrappers

3. Fine-tuned models someone else trained
